How do I export a WhatsApp chat?
  1. Open the group in WhatsApp.
  2. Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat. iPhone: tap the group name → Export chat.
  3. Pick Include media if you want voice notes turned into text, otherwise Without media.
  4. Save it to Files or e-mail it to yourself, then choose it here.
